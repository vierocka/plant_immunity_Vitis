Comparison of classical WGCNA topology and the original hub-anchored network under protected and unprotected ComBat correction. Top row: scale-free-topology fit as a function of the signed WGCNA soft-thresholding power. The dashed line marks the approximate R² = 0.85 criterion; β = 8 was used for direct comparison because the fit plateaued near this value in both matrices. Middle row: median gene connectivity at each soft-thresholding power. Bottom row: module-size distributions for the original hub-anchored correlation-neighborhood method, shown as module rank from largest to smallest. Dashed lines indicate the median module size. The original method produced 147 protected and 155 unprotected modules, whereas classical WGCNA produced 53 and 51 broad genome-wide modules, respectively. WGCNA module sizes are reported as the number of genes assigned to each hard-partition module.
